The ChA rectangle crosses a survey boundary. Compare only its Union map unit with the Union USDA fixture. Do not apply the Union fixture to the Stanly area. Verify each ZIP hash with `sha256sum`. Extract each `.shp` file from its ZIP, then read four little-endian double-precision values at byte offset 112. These values are the exported west, south, east, and north bounds. The `.prj` member declares WGS84. All three files contain one rectangle.

**WSS settings.** The capture took place on November 14, 2026. Use the WSS application at `https://websoilsurvey.nrcs.usda.gov/app/WebSoilSurvey.aspx`. Select Soil Data Explorer, Suitabilities and Limitations for Use, and the three interpretations below. Keep the displayed default rating options: Dominant Condition aggregation, no component percent cutoff, and Higher tie-break. Include the map-unit table, component breakdown, reasons, and numeric values. The WSS AOI view reports Union tabular version 27, dated Sep 3, 2026, and spatial version 8, dated Aug 27, 2025. It reports Stanly tabular version 28, dated Aug 31, 2026, and spatial version 13, dated Aug 28, 2025. The WSS map warns that this display scale exceeds the source map scale of 1:24,000. Do not use this AOI to claim exact site boundaries.

**Comparison.** The captured `internal/soil/testdata/union-wss-mapunit-identities-raw.json` response identifies AeB as MUKEY `115328`, ApB as `115330`, and ChA as `115341`. For each use, WSS shows Dominant Condition, no cutoff, and Higher tie-break. The captured `sdvattribute` data reports Dominant Condition, null cutoff, and tie-break `1`. The following rating classes agree between WSS and the captured `muaggatt` rows:

WSS reports 11.1 acres of AeB, 11.1 acres of ApB, and 5.2 acres of ChA in their separate AOIs. The ChA area is 41.9% of its whole AOI. Do not compare these AOI acres with the parcel assessment acres.

**Components and reasons.** For AeB, WSS shows Ailey 60% with slow water movement for septic and thick cemented-pan depth for dwellings without basements. The captured component rows contain those labels and ratings. WSS shows Ailey 60% and Appling 25% for the `Not limited` dwelling-with-basement class; the captured component rows retain both components. For ApB, WSS shows Appling 90% and slow water movement for septic. The captured rows retain that component and reason. WSS shows Appling 90% with `Not limited` for each dwelling use. The WSS table shows only components with the displayed map-unit rating; it does not prove that other components are absent. For each of the three uses in ChA, WSS and captured `cointerp` rows list Chewacla 87%, Congaree 8%, and Wehadkee 5%. WSS calls the last component `Wehadkee, undrained`; the captured component name is `Wehadkee`. For septic, both sources list flooding and depth to saturated zone for these components. They also list bottom-layer seepage for Chewacla and Wehadkee, and slow water movement for all three. WSS displays the slow-water numeric value as 0.50; the captured row stores 0.498. For dwellings without basements, both sources show flooding for all three components and depth to saturated zone for Chewacla and Wehadkee. For dwellings with basements, both sources show flooding and depth to saturated zone for all three. WSS displays the Congaree depth value as 0.90; the captured row stores 0.899. These numeric differences are consistent with display rounding. Do not change the captured source values.

**Limits.** The captured USDA `sacatalog` response reports NC179 survey version 28, with a raw save time of Sep 3, 2026. WSS reports NC179 tabular version 27 on the same calendar date. A new read-only USDA catalog query returned version 28 for all three keys again. The WSS and USDA services report different version numbers for NC179. The cause is not verified; do not claim that their complete source datasets match. Agreement of the sampled rating classes does not prove that all values match between versions. The ChA AOI includes other Union map-unit symbols and part of another survey area. The ApB AOI includes a WSS ApC row with acreage rounded to 0.0. Do not read 0.0 as proof of zero intersection. This comparison checks all three sample Union keys and three uses. It does not verify site suitability or septic approval.

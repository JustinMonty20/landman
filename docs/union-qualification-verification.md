## Final independent spatial and refresh corrections
The 5 km-edge fixture proved that projecting a shortest segment selected in EPSG:3857 can overstate the minimum EPSG:2264 distance by 1.20917 ft. For disjoint parcel/road and county-edge measurements, complete EPSG:3857 source shapes are segmentized to at most 10 m per edge before transforming to EPSG:2264. The parcel and road are then compared as full projected shapes with `ST_Distance`. Stored 3857 intersection still gives exact zero contact. The 3857 indexed, 8 km candidate filter remains in place. The 10 m and 1 m subdivision references agree in the 5 km winner, tie, and 5278.88746 ft radius cases to less than 0.001 ft, below the 0.01 ft tie window; no clearance clamp is applied, so 1 mm and 0.2 m disjoint gaps stay positive. This bounded subdivision is an approximation to curved projected edges, not a mathematically exact curved-edge minimum for arbitrary geometries.

Current zoning method `stored-overlay-measured/4` retains full-resolution stored source/parcel shapes and raw fingerprints. Analysis copies use an EPSG:3857 0.000001 m grid via `ST_ReducePrecision`; intersection, union, and difference use that grid. The captured county metadata declares 0.001 m XY tolerance and 10000 XY units per meter, both coarser than the analysis grid. Source polygons are unioned and pairwise conflicts classified before a single parcel clip, preventing separately clipped diagonal contacts from inventing conflicts or oblique parcel cuts from inventing gaps. Positive-area hits collapsed by the declared grid fail reconciliation rather than passing as absent. No area epsilon classifies conflicts. Completed gaps/conflicts are measured after at most 10 m source-edge subdivision and EPSG:2264 projection. Hits covering more than half the parcel measure as parcel acreage minus projected actual complement; smaller hits measure directly to avoid subtracting nearly equal large areas. Both acres and shares use the same measured hit area. The 10 mm by 1 mm and 0.5 m² notches remain positive gaps with coherent 0..1 shares. The 10 mm notch fixture checks a 2e-10 dimensionless share difference against a 1 m subdivision reference; this fixture-specific measurement tolerance is not a universal area error bound. This shared-representation arithmetic is not exact geodesic area. The grid changes topology at sub-micrometer features and projected-edge subdivision has shape-dependent error; neither guarantees arbitrary source precision or county-scale performance.
Refresh failure observation is a standalone short monotonic UPSERT of the original attempt start in `qualification_refresh_failures.failed_at`. Publication does not read, lock, or delete the failure table. Freshness exposes a failure only when its attempt began at or after the **current head's** `started_at`, including zoning as a road boundary dependency. A physically retained older failure is superseded by a newer head; a newer failed gate remains visible even when an older publisher is blocked on reconciliation's head-row lock. Postcallback gate errors retain their original start. Persistence errors are joined into the returned error, although a database outage cannot guarantee a recorded status. Qualification hole boundary contacts may occur at edge interiors, but shared edges, multiple contacts, exterior vertices, and concave crossings remain invalid. Parcel ring parser is unchanged. Saved `measurement-*.log` receipts describe the prior head, not this correction; see `stability-*.log` and its final-head receipt.

The failed premise was that separately projected polygon pieces preserve adjacency and coverage. New persisted zoning and jurisdiction overlay, coverage, conflict, and gap topology uses source EPSG:3857. Only the completed measurement geometry is simplified at zero tolerance to remove redundant collinear vertices and transformed to EPSG:2264. Parcel and hit acreage use that same measurement representation. A stored positive-area conflict is persisted independently of measured acreage to classify split parcels. Road nearest segments are computed before transformation, and only their lengths are measured in EPSG:2264. An explicit null jurisdiction `NAME` remains unknown, returns a null name in evidence, and its positive covered area is ambiguous, not complete named coverage. A point-touching qualification hole is accepted without changing parcel ring assembly. Failed county-host lock acquisition records source-refresh failure against the old published generation. An older successful attempt cannot erase a later failure.

PostGIS fixtures assert zero invented gaps and conflicts for shared-edge midpoint splits and exact-cover extra vertices in both orientations. They cover a valid 1 mm strip, duplicate districts, parcel holes, and a positive 1 cm² conflict. Road fixtures keep 1 mm clearance positive beside a short edge and 0.2 m clearance positive beside a 5 km edge. Freshness fixtures cover unknown jurisdiction names, blocked county-lock timeout and cancellation, and a newer failure surviving an older publication. Parser fixtures reject crossed and exterior holes and accept a one-vertex touch. The ignored `artifacts/verify/union-qualification/topology-*.log` files retain focused results, migration checks, the offline race suite, the PostGIS race CI lane, staticcheck, vet, and build.
